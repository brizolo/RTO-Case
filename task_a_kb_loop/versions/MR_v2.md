Supervisor answers - Meridia (MR)

Answers approved after the original knowledge base was written. Where they differ, these answers are the more recent ones.

Q: How much is the bag deposit now?
A: From 1 October the thermal bag deposit is 3,000 MRD (was 2,500). Payment options are the same: at the Rider Centre or deducted from the first four weekly payouts.

Q: When do I get paid?
A: Payouts are weekly. Earnings from Monday to Sunday are paid into the MiraPay wallet on the following Tuesday.

Q: Does my phone need to be Android?
A: No. The courier app runs on Android 9 or later and iOS 15 or later. The phone must belong to the courier.

Q: I was rejected. Can I just sign up again with a different email?
A: No. Applications with a new email but the same ID are detected as duplicates and closed. The only option is to reapply with the original email once 30 days have passed.

Q: My application was rejected. Can I apply again?
A: Yes. If the application itself was rejected (not just a document), a new application can be submitted 30 days after the rejection date, using the same email address and the same link. Before 30 days the system will block it.

Q: Do I have to pay for the training session?
A: No, the Rider Centre session is free.
