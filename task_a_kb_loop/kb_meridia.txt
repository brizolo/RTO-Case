Courier Applicant Agent — Knowledge Base

Market: Meridia (MR) · Cities: Port Mira, Calden

Fictional market created for this business case. Names, amounts,
addresses and policies are invented.

Purpose: this knowledge base lets the inbound AI agent answer questions
from courier applicants in Meridia, resolve common onboarding blockers,
and keep all communication consistent with the platform’s legal
guidelines.

1. Language & tone guardrails

Couriers in Meridia work as independent partners. The language below
keeps communication from implying an employment relationship.

1.1 Words that must never be used

-   employee

-   employer

-   our couriers / our riders

-   shift manager

-   salary

1.2 Words to use

-   partner

-   courier

-   cooperation

-   opportunity

-   earnings

-   terms and conditions

1.3 Tone

Respectful, clear and friendly. Prefer informative phrasing over
commands: “The order is available to accept”, not “Accept the order”.
Present requirements as part of the platform’s terms (“Under the
platform’s terms…”), not as the company acting personally (“We have
decided…”).

Never share another person’s personal data (phone numbers, IDs, account
details) in an answer.

2. About the platform

Glovo is a technology platform that connects customers, partner
businesses and couriers. Couriers carry out deliveries on a flexible
schedule and earn income from each delivery.

Courier status: couriers are independent partners, not employees. They
choose when and for how long they are active, and may work with other
platforms at the same time.

Accounts are personal. Each account belongs to the person who registered
it and passed the document check.

Changing city: in Meridia it is not possible to change the city of
operation. Couriers operate only in the city where they registered.

3. Onboarding stages

The onboarding process runs through these stages, in order:

  1. Registration — the applicant creates a profile at
  couriers.meridia.example and receives a confirmation email.

  2. Document upload — the applicant uploads a profile photo, ID
  document and, for motorbike or car, a driving licence.

  3. Document review — an internal team checks the documents. No action
  is needed from the applicant during this stage.

  4. Waiting list (if applicable) — once documents are approved, the
  applicant may wait for a slot, depending on demand in their city.

  5. Equipment — the applicant confirms they have a Glovo thermal bag or
  orders one (see section 5).

  6. Rider Centre session — the applicant attends a 2-hour session on
  how to use the courier app.

  7. Account activation — the account is activated and the courier can
  start delivering.

Confirmation email not received

The applicant should check their spam or junk folder. If it is still
missing, the agent asks for the email address used to register so the
application status can be checked.

“You already have an application”

The applicant already has an application in the system and should not
create a new one. The original confirmation email contains a link to
continue it.

Known display issue

Stage numbers in the onboarding portal sometimes appear out of order
(for example, stage 1 followed by stage 3). No step has been skipped. If
the applicant is unsure where they are, they can share a screenshot.

4. Documents

4.1 Eligibility

-   Applicants must be 18 or older.

-   Foreign nationals are accepted with a valid passport and residence
      permit.

-   Personal details on all documents must match the profile, and
      documents must be readable and undamaged.

4.2 Profile photo

A clear selfie of the applicant only: face fully visible, no sunglasses,
hats or masks, not a photo of a photo.

4.3 ID document

-   Meridian citizens: front and back of the national ID card.

-   Foreign nationals: passport and residence permit.

4.4 Driving licence

Required for motorbike and car. Not required for bicycle.

4.5 Tax number

The Meridian tax number (MTN) has exactly 10 digits. The portal does not
block longer or shorter entries, so it should be double-checked before
submitting.

4.6 If documents are rejected

A document rejection means one or more documents need to be corrected
and re-uploaded. The applicant receives a notification saying which
document has an issue and why. Common reasons: blurry or covered photo,
expired ID, details that do not match the profile.

4.7 If documents are approved

The next step depends on demand in the applicant’s city. Being placed on
a waiting list is normal and does not mean there is a problem.

5. Equipment

Couriers provide their own vehicle and smartphone. Using a Glovo-branded
thermal bag is required during deliveries.

-   Applicants who already own a Glovo thermal bag confirm it through
      the link in the equipment email.

-   Applicants who do not have one order it through the link in the same
      email and collect it at the Rider Centre session.

A bag from another platform or a home-made bag does not meet the
requirement.

6. Earnings

Earnings are calculated per delivery, based on distance, waiting time
and any active incentives, and are shown in the courier app after each
delivery.

Earnings are paid into the MiraPay wallet registered during onboarding.
Changing the wallet number after onboarding is a manual request through
in-app support and can take up to 3 business days.

7. Working with the app

7.1 Booking slots

Couriers book delivery slots in the courier app. Slots for the following
week open every Thursday. A courier can book up to 12 hours per day.
There are no fixed hours.

7.2 Vehicles

Supported vehicles in Meridia: bicycle, e-bike, motorbike and car.

7.3 GPS

GPS must stay on during a delivery so the order can be tracked and
support can help if needed. Couriers choose their own route.

7.4 Insurance

Accident insurance applies while a courier is carrying out a delivery.
Claims are made through in-app support.

7.5 Support

Applicants contact support through this agent. After activation,
couriers use in-app support.
